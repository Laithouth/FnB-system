Q1

How big is the effect when meaning is held fixed?

Sections 3–4. Output shifts with wording and format; people who happen to phrase well pull ahead — and the least experienced gain most.

FIGURE 1 · SCLAR ET AL. [2]

Formatting alone moves accuracy

Only spacing, capitalization and similar surface features changed. The spread did not shrink with larger or instruction-tuned models.

FIGURE 2 · DELL'ACQUA ET AL. [3]

AI assistance closes the skill gap

Consultants with no coding background reached 84% with AI — nearly the 86% average. But the gain was not retained once the tool was removed.

FIGURE 3 · BRYNJOLFSSON, LI & RAYMOND [4]

Least experienced gain the most

Assisted agents hit in 2 months what took unassisted agents 8. Manager escalations fell 25%.

FIGURE 4 · USER SURVEY [5], n = 243

People know prompts matter — and still struggle

Mean satisfaction with AI output: 3.24 / 5. Most respondents reported no formal prompt training.

READING THE TWO QUESTIONS TOGETHER · SECTION 7

Three patterns

HIGH CONFIDENCESensitivity explains the gap.

If phrasing moves output by tens of points, good phrasers outperform — exactly what two field settings measured.

REPLICATED TWICENovices benefit most.

The strongest argument for teaching prompting broadly, not as a specialist skill.

UNTESTEDDurability is unproven.

Gains vanished without the tool [3]; the teaching study measured literacy, not task performance, with no control group [6].

    What the next study would need

    Closing the gap the paper names

    1. Random assignment to prompting instruction vs. an equal-time control course (fixes the gap in [6]).
    2. Outcome measured as task performance, not self-rated literacy.
    3. A delayed post-test without AI access, weeks after instruction ends (the test [3] ran for assistance).
    4. Participants spanning experience levels, to see whether novices still gain most.
